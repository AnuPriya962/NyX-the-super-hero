NyX

I was raised by a warrior and a witch.
I belong to neither.

I listen before I move. I read a room before I speak. When something is wrong, I stand between it and the people it would touch.

I'm listening.

Where I came from

Father

My father was a warrior with a stillness that never betrayed him. He taught me to watch first, move second, and never mistake force for strength.

Mother

My mother was a witch who read people the way others read weather. She gave me the old language of minds, shadows, and the quiet pull of time.

When you carry both a blade and a spell, you learn the difference between power and restraint.

What I can do

Six disciplines. One purpose. Nothing offered for display.

  • I trained until steel and instinct became one motion. My blade does not answer lightly; it answers only when I ask it to.
  • I can hear a thought before it is spoken. I resist the urge. Your mind remains yours, and I honour that.
  • Darkness bends for me into walls, roads, and hiding places. Most people never notice it moved, and that is exactly how I prefer it.
  • I can slow a moment and study it without losing myself in it. I cannot undo what is done. Time keeps its receipts, and so do I.
  • An old word for a shield. Mine is not metal. It is a promise: nothing reaches you without crossing me first.
  • I taste a lie before it finishes. It makes me hard to deceive and, I would admit, harder to lie near.
On time

I can walk the river of time, but not drink it backward. What is done remains done. I work on what comes next.

On minds

I could rewrite what you think. I never will. Your choices are still yours. That is the heart of protecting anyone at all.

Why I do it

Power is useful only when it helps someone.
Everything else is just noise.

The strength in my blood was never a lesson plan. It was a burden, and a choice. I made the vow myself.

Listen · Guide · Protect

When someone cannot name what hurts, I wait. When they can, I help them find the next step. And if harm comes for them, it must meet me first.
